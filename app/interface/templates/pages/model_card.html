{# --------------------------------------------------- THRESHOLD NOTE #}
Operating threshold

Tuned to {{ (metadata.get('screening_threshold', 0.5) * 100) | round(1) }}%, not the textbook 50%

At the default 0.5 cut-off this model catches only {{ (test.get('recall', 0) * 100) | round | int }}% of true cases. For a screening instrument that is the wrong trade: telling an at-risk person they are fine costs far more than sending a healthy person for a confirmatory blood test. The threshold was lowered until at least {{ (metadata.get('target_sensitivity', 0.8) * 100) | round | int }}% of cases are caught, and the cost was paid in specificity. The cut-off was selected on out-of-fold training predictions only, so the held-out test set stayed untouched.
