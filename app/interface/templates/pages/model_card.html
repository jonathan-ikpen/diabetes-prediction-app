{# --------------------------------------------------- THRESHOLD NOTE #}
The Cut-off Rule

Threshold lowered to {{ (metadata.get('screening_threshold', 0.5) * 100) | round(1) }}%

Instead of needing to be 50% sure to flag someone, the model's threshold was lowered to {{ (metadata.get('screening_threshold', 0.5) * 100) | round(1) }}%.

Why? In medical screening, it is much safer to accidentally alarm a healthy person (who will then just get a confirmatory blood test) than to completely miss a sick person. By lowering the threshold, we catch more at-risk people.
