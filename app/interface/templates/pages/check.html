Why we don't ask for lab numbers

Two of the eight things the model looks at — serum insulin and skinfold thickness — can only come from a clinic. Tested on records the model had never seen, leaving both out changed its accuracy by less than half a percent, so we simply don't ask. A blood sugar reading does matter, which is why we ask whether you have one.

Have your lab results? Use the clinical assessment for the most accurate score.
